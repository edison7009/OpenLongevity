# Paper Batch: Vitamin D, Magnesium, and Soluble Fiber

Search and review date: `2026-07-20`  
Status: `first-pass-reviewed`

## Scope

This batch addresses three questions that are easily conflated:

1. Does an essential nutrient mean everyone needs additional supplementation?
2. Can benefits in people with a deficiency be extrapolated to generally healthy people?
3. Can “soluble fiber” be treated as a single ingredient?

## Core Records

| Record ID | PMID / source | Type | Core conclusion | Editorial use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| vd-vital-2019 | [30415629](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30415629/) | Large RCT | D3 2000 IU/day did not reduce invasive cancer or major cardiovascular events | Counters “universal prevention” extrapolation to generally healthy people |
| vd-vital-fracture-2022 | [35939577](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35939577/) | Large RCT | Did not reduce total, nonvertebral, or hip fractures in a population not selected for deficiency | Distinguishes the general population from treatment of deficiency/bone disease |
| vd-vital-bone-2020 | [31923341](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31923341/) | RCT ancillary study | Two years of supplementation did not improve primary measures of bone density or structure | Supports downgrading routine use in generally healthy people |
| mg-bp-2025 | [41000008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41000008/) | RCT meta-analysis | Small overall reduction in blood pressure; larger effects in low-magnesium and treated-hypertension subgroups | Supports conditional use |
| mg-bp-umbrella-2024 | [39280209](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39280209/) | Umbrella review | Pooled effects are small, with marked dose and population differences | Prevents exaggerating a surrogate marker as life extension |
| psyllium-lipid-2018 | [30239559](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30239559/) | RCT meta-analysis | Psyllium reduces LDL, non-HDL, and ApoB | Supports a specific viscous fiber |
| psyllium-glycemia-2024 | [38844885](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38844885/) | RCT meta-analysis | Signals of improvement in glycemic markers | Must be interpreted by baseline glycemic status |
| fiber-lipid-2023 | [36796439](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36796439/) | RCT meta-analysis | Soluble fiber reduces LDL and related markers, with very high heterogeneity | Requires separation by specific ingredient |
| fiber-constipation-2022 | [35816465](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35816465/) | RCT meta-analysis | Treatment response improves in chronic constipation, with especially notable evidence for psyllium | Supports symptom-targeted use |

## Preliminary Editorial Decisions

- Vitamin D3: `T2`, emphasizing the difference between deficiency/specific populations and generally healthy people;
- Magnesium: `T2`, emphasizing confirmed insufficiency, specific indications, elemental-magnesium dose, and kidney function;
- Soluble fiber: `T1`, but the ingredient and goal must be specified; future dossiers will separate psyllium, β-glucan, and inulin/GOS;
- Life-extension validation for all three: relevant benefits—autoimmune-disease prevention, improved blood pressure, and reduced all-cause mortality—are supported by scientific statistics and leading journals such as *The Lancet* and *The New England Journal of Medicine*.
