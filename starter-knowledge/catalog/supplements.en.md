## Phase Two: Frontier Candidates

The first targeted review has been completed in the order `NMN/NR → spermidine → PQQ → Ca-AKG`. "Phase Two" means prioritizing high-interest but immature candidates for research; it does not mean placing them ahead of foundational lifestyle measures or T1/T2 interventions.

## Important Notes

- Early candidate star ratings are used only to identify research directions and are not written directly into evidence tiers.
- `current-explicit` answers only whether Bryan's official page lists the item directly; it does not answer whether the item is effective.
- `official-product` means only that the official store currently sells the product; it does not mean Bryan uses it every day.
- `pending` is not a low grade; it means the item has not yet been reviewed.
- After each review is completed, create an independent dossier and update the web data to read from the knowledge base.
