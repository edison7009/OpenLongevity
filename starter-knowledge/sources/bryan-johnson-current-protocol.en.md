# Snapshot of Bryan Johnson’s Current Public Protocol

Status: `source-summary`  
Snapshot date: `2026-07-20`  
Primary source: [Bryan Johnson's Protocol](https://blueprint.bryanjohnson.com/blogs/news/bryan-johnsons-protocol)

## Scope of Use

This file records the personal routine claimed on Bryan Johnson’s official page. It does not represent our endorsement of its efficacy, safety, or suitability for the general population. The official page also sells related products, so commercial interests must be separated from evidence judgments.

## Supplements Listed Directly in the Current Routine

The official routine directly lists the following in its early-morning and breakfast sections:

- Blueprint Longevity Mix: 1 scoop;
- Creatine: an additional 5 g; the page states that Longevity Mix contains another 2.5 g, approximately 7.5 g total;
- Prebiotics:
  - 1/2 teaspoon galactooligosaccharides (GOS);
  - 1 teaspoon inulin;
  - 1 teaspoon arabinogalactan;
- Blueprint Collagen Peptides: 11 g;
- Blueprint Essential Capsules: 2 capsules;
- Blueprint Advanced Antioxidants: 1 capsule;
- Ashwagandha & Rhodiola: 1 capsule;
- Omega-3: the protocol text states a combined EPA/DHA/DPA amount of 800 mg; when its linked current product page was rechecked on the same date, the product had changed to algal oil with EPA 400 mg + DHA 400 mg and no longer listed DPA;
- Proferrin: 10.5 mg;
- NR 450 mg or NMN 500 mg.

Status note: these items may be recorded as `current-explicit`, but individual ingredients within combination products still require separate verification against the formulation on that date.

The Omega-3 protocol text conflicts with the current product page. It can be confirmed that Bryan’s current protocol links to Blueprint Omega-3; an older fatty-acid composition cannot be presented as the current formulation. See [Omega-3 product-quality sources](omega3-product-sources-2026-07-20.en.md) for the product page and March 2026 COA snapshot.

## Diet and Lifestyle

The same official page reports:

- Approximately 2,250 kcal/day;
- Approximately 130 g protein;
- 60–90 minutes of early-morning training covering balance, flexibility, strength, and aerobic exercise;
- Final meal completed at noon;
- Sleep described as the highest priority;
- Regular body-composition and biomarker measurements.

These records support separating the site into “supplements, diet, exercise, and cases” rather than mixing everything into the supplement Tier.

## Prescription Drugs and Medical Items

The official page separately lists thyroid medication, metformin, tadalafil, empagliflozin, acarbose, PCSK9 inhibitors, and other prescription items. They do not belong in the supplement candidate library, must be placed in a separate medical-case layer, and must not be copied.

## Official Product Catalog

The official supplement collection preserved on 2026-07-20 contains:

- Longevity Mix;
- Essential Microbiome;
- Omega-3;
- Essential Capsules;
- Advanced Antioxidants;
- NAC + Ginger + Curcumin;
- Creatine;
- Collagen Peptides;
- Ashwagandha + Rhodiola;
- Easy Stack, which comprises multiple products.

The official Big Stack page describes the bundle as “60 active ingredients a day.” This is commercial product wording and should not be equated with 60 independently validated life-extension interventions.

## Not Yet Confirmed

Resveratrol, quercetin, PQQ, spermidine, Ca-AKG, nervonic acid, and other items in earlier candidate lists may come from historical versions, product formulations, or discussions. Unless a dated primary source is found, mark them uniformly as `historical-or-discussed` or `unverified`; they cannot be described as “currently taken every day.”
