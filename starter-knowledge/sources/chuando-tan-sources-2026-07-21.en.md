## Conflict Record

### Eggs

- Older 2017 interview: six poached eggs, only two yolks;
- CNA 2023: breakfast may be a protein shake, or oats, eggs, honey, and avocado;
- Esquire HK 2026: six eggs in the morning, only two yolks, sometimes with avocado.

Conclusion: “six eggs, two yolks” can be confirmed in a 2026 interview, but it cannot be expanded into an unchanging practice of eating six whole boiled eggs every day for years.

### Training frequency

- The Straits Times 2026: aims for five times per week, but realistically usually about three;
- Esquire HK 2026: four to five times per week.

Conclusion: stated and actual practice may change over time; an exact, invariant weekly plan must not be invented.

### Sugar

Direct interviews support reducing processed foods and drinking only water, but also mention honey, blueberries, durian, and occasional ice cream. The conclusion should be “limits sugary drinks and processed foods,” not “completely sugar-free.”

### Supplements and fasting

The reliable interviews in this batch do not disclose specific use of NAD⁺, PQQ, Omega-3, or vitamins, nor a seven-hour pre-sleep fast. Status remains `unverified`.
