# NAD⁺ Metabolic Pathway Source Batch

Research date: `2026-07-20`  
Evidence cutoff: `2026-07-20`  
Scope: NAD⁺ basic physiology, niacin nutrition, age-related change, NR/NMN, direct oral NAD⁺, and intravenous NAD⁺.

## Source Strategy

- Nutritional requirements, foods, deficiency, and high-dose risks: prioritize the NIH Office of Dietary Supplements;
- Human aging and interventions: PubMed reviews, systematic reviews, randomized trials, and human pharmacokinetics;
- Direct infusion: distinguish wellness use, specific-disease trials, and commercial retrospective data;
- Do not present mechanisms, blood markers, or use by public figures as healthspan benefits.

## Targeted Search

```text
(NAD+ OR nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) AND
(human aging OR clinical trial OR systematic review)

(intravenous NAD+ OR oral NAD+) AND
(human OR pharmacokinetics OR safety OR randomized)

(niacin OR nicotinamide OR tryptophan) AND
(NAD OR deficiency OR pellagra)
```

## Key Boundaries

- Values in the NIH food table count niacin in the food itself and do not include conversion from tryptophan;
- NR/NMN increases in blood NAD-related markers are assessed separately from tissue-level improvement;
- Direct oral NAD⁺ cannot borrow human results from NR/NMN;
- An intravenous NAD⁺ heart-failure trial applies to that disease context; anti-aging infusion in healthy people is evaluated separately;
- NAD⁺ itself is a human molecule and does not receive a scientific Tier; specific products and routes enter the candidate list and dossiers separately.
