# Spermidine Dossier Audit

::: warning Audit conclusion
The updated dossier can serve as a cautious, traceable research note. It presents the negative one-year primary cognitive endpoint, limited short-term exposure at 40 mg, and a 2026 pilot signal for vaccine response in 40 participants. The new signal is not extrapolated to general anti-aging effects. Interactions, long-term risks, and confirmatory trials remain insufficient, so the audit stays `partial`.
:::

## Blocking Issues

- Long-term interaction and complex-disease data are lacking;
- The 2026 vaccine-response signal has not been independently confirmed;
- Trial data inform effective dose, monitoring, and long-term duration for the general population.

## Non-blocking Improvements

- Obtain full text and the complete adverse-event table for the 40 mg/day study;
- Track confirmatory immune and cognition trials;
- Review specific spermidine products separately under the P1–P3 quality framework.
