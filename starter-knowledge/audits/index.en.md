## How to Read the Audits

- `PASS` does not mean a conclusion will remain correct forever;
- `PARTIAL` means content exists but the evidence chain or scope of applicability is incomplete;
- `FAIL` means an applicable item is missing and requires further research or explicit identification as unknown;
- Audit counts are used only to manage work, not to score supplements;
- Tier and audit status are two independent axes.

See the [intervention dossier audit template](../templates/intervention-audit.md) and the [localized AI4L audit method](../methods/ai4l-adaptation.en.md).
