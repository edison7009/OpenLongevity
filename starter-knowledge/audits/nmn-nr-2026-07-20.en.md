# NMN / NR Dossier Audit

::: warning Audit conclusion
The current dossier is suitable as a research note on an emerging candidate. It places NMN and NR alongside each other without conflating them, highlights the negative muscle-function meta-analysis and negative NR cognition trial, and records corporate relationships and ongoing trials. It cannot be used to prescribe an individual dose; long-term safety, interactions, and boundaries for specific diseases remain insufficient.
:::

## Blocking Issues

The following prevent use of the dossier for individual clinical decisions but do not overturn the current `T3`:

- Reliable long-term interaction and complex-disease data are lacking;
- Short-term tolerability cannot establish safety over years of use;
- There is currently no stable functional benefit, so an “effective dose” cannot be prescribed from NAD markers.

## Non-blocking Improvements

- Complete the sex composition and full adverse-event table for every included trial;
- Check the preregistered primary endpoint when NCT04691986 publishes results;
- Create separate product-quality dossiers for NMN and NR.
