# Ca-AKG Dossier Audit

::: warning Audit conclusion
The dossier is suitable as an initial research map for Ca-AKG. It does not extrapolate mouse lifespan findings to humans or describe a combination product, a cross-sectional study, or a DNA methylation clock as “age reversal.” Two registered RCTs are documented and their efficacy results are awaited. The current T4 rating is reasonable; long-term safety, interactions, and confirmatory human results remain to be added.
:::

## Blocking Issues

- The two registered RCTs have not published verifiable efficacy results;
- Long-term safety, adverse-event frequency, and drug interactions are insufficiently characterized;
- A proprietary methylation clock cannot directly establish function, disease, or lifespan.

## Non-blocking Improvements

- When RCT results are published, re-audit prespecified endpoints, attrition, and adverse events;
- Compare human pharmacokinetics of Ca-AKG, ordinary AKG, and sustained-release formulations;
- Move specific products, elemental calcium, and vitamin formulations into P1–P3 product dossiers.
