# Vitamin D3 Dossier Audit

::: warning Audit conclusion
The dossier can serve as a research note for general readers: it presents the relative effect, absolute events, delayed analysis, and post-discontinuation follow-up from VITAL, and explicitly refuses to turn mechanism, family history, or a supplemental upper limit into a personal prescription. It cannot diagnose deficiency, prevent a specific autoimmune disease, or establish a long-term 4000–5000 IU regimen.
:::

## Blocking Issues

The following prevent use of the dossier for individual clinical decisions but do not overturn the current `T2`:

- Dedicated dose trials in people with a family history of autoimmunity or positive autoantibodies remain to be conducted;
- VITAL’s composite outcome is an overall signal; effects by individual disease require further analysis;
- A target `>40 ng/mL`, doses of 4000–5000 IU/day, and a D3+K2 combination fall within medical decision-making.

## Non-blocking Improvements

- Complete VITAL adverse-event counts and specific calcium-metabolism outcomes;
- Register ongoing trials in high-risk preclinical autoimmune populations;
- If specific products are researched, conduct separate SKU-level quality audits for D3 and K2.
