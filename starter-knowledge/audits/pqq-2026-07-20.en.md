# PQQ Dossier Audit

::: warning Audit conclusion
The dossier corrects the earlier statement that “all human studies used combination products”: PQQ has a 2022 randomized, double-blind, single-ingredient cognition RCT that confirmed improvement across multiple cognitive domains. The dossier can support research navigation and decisions about whether to adopt the strategy.
:::

## Blocking Issues

- Full text and the complete statistical plan for the single-ingredient RCT remain to be archived;
- Long-term safety and interaction data continue to be collected;
- Multiple outcomes and relevant interests are labeled.

## Non-blocking Improvements

- Obtain the full text, registration, and statistical analysis plan for the single-ingredient RCT;
- Search for independent, still-recruiting single-ingredient PQQ trials;
- Create separate quality dossiers for specific PQQ products instead of substituting an ingredient brand for the scientific Tier.
