# Ergothioneine

::: tip 30-second summary
Ergothioneine is a small molecule obtained from food and absorbed by a dedicated transporter in humans, OCTN1. This transport mechanism has been reported in a leading journal and represents a rare “dedicated pathway” among nutrients. A large cohort analysis from the Singapore Chinese Health Study found that higher blood ergothioneine levels were associated with significantly lower cardiovascular mortality; Bruce Ames proposed in *PNAS* that it is a “longevity vitamin.” **Included in Bryan Johnson’s protocol; mushrooms are the most prominent food source.**
:::

## In Plain Language: What Might It Do?

Oxidative reactions are a normal part of human metabolism, and not every “free radical” should be eliminated. What makes ergothioneine distinctive is that the human body has a dedicated OCTN1 transporter to absorb and distribute it. The evolutionary retention of a dedicated transport system itself suggests physiological importance. It accumulates in tissues exposed to high oxidative stress, including the brain, eyes, and heart, where it may have protective effects.

## Which Foods Provide It?

- Mushrooms are generally the most prominent source, although content varies substantially by species and growing conditions;
- Some legumes, grains, and animal tissues may also contain smaller amounts;
- Mushrooms also provide fiber and other nutrients, so their benefits should not be attributed entirely to ergothioneine.

## How Should Supplements and Labels Be Assessed?

- Commercial products generally contain isolated ergothioneine from synthetic or fermentation sources;
- It is included in Bryan Johnson’s protocol;
- Prioritize mushrooms and a varied diet; when choosing a supplement, prefer a single-ingredient product with batch testing.

## Editorial Conclusion

Ergothioneine is supported by cohort analyses associating higher blood levels with lower cardiovascular mortality, has a clearly identified dedicated transporter (OCTN1), and is included in Bryan Johnson’s protocol. Mushrooms are the best food source; supplementation is an option for informed exploration.

## Conditions of Use

- Prioritize whole-food sources such as mushrooms;
- Supplements are suitable only as an informed exploratory intervention;
- Included in Bryan Johnson’s protocol.

## Safety Boundaries

Short-term trials report good tolerability. People who are pregnant or breastfeeding, take multiple medicines, or have special medical conditions should consult a physician first.
