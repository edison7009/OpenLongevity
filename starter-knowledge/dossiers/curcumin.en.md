## Core Conclusion

Curcumin refers to a group of polyphenols in turmeric. Its most usable human evidence is for short-term relief of pain and stiffness and improved function in knee osteoarthritis. It may modestly change CRP, fasting glucose, and some metabolic markers, but it has not been shown to extend life or prevent cancer, cardiovascular events, or cognitive decline.

## Turmeric, Curcumin, and Enhanced Formulations Are Not the Same

### 1. Turmeric as Food

Turmeric powder is a spice and only a fraction consists of curcuminoids. Food seasoning and concentrated extracts are different exposures. In the public protocol, [Bryan Johnson](#/person/bryan-johnson) uses about 5 g of turmeric powder with black pepper; that does not endorse any capsule brand in the table.

### 2. Standardized Curcumin Extracts

The label should state actual curcuminoid content and standardization. Anti-inflammatory and antioxidant findings in laboratory systems explain why the compound is studied; they do not substitute for human functional or clinical outcomes.

### 3. Phytosomes, Fenugreek Carriers, and Piperine

These technologies may improve absorption, but pharmacokinetic findings apply to the specific formulation studied. Greater blood exposure does not automatically mean stronger joint benefit or greater longevity. Piperine may also alter drug metabolism and is not appropriate for everyone.

## Human Evidence

### Knee Osteoarthritis

A 2024 umbrella analysis of 11 existing meta-analyses found lower pain and stiffness and better WOMAC function with curcumin. The overall direction is favorable, but source trials were usually short and varied in formulation and dose. This evidence does not replace diagnosis, exercise rehabilitation, weight management, or standard medicines.

### Inflammatory and Metabolic Markers

A 2024 meta-analysis covering 103 randomized trials and 7,216 participants reported statistically significant changes in fasting glucose, CRP, HDL, weight, and some other markers. Evidence for many other outcomes was moderate, low, or very low certainty. Marker changes do not establish fewer myocardial infarctions, cancers, dementia cases, or deaths.

### Longevity and Healthspan

There is no reliable randomized trial with lifespan or all-cause mortality as the target, and no evidence that long-term supplementation prevents aging in generally healthy people. Curcumin is better framed as a conditional tool for a defined symptom target than as a foundational longevity supplement.

## How to Choose and Track It

- Define the target first; do not add it automatically for antioxidant marketing when no joint symptom or other clear goal exists;
- Record the exact formulation, actual curcuminoid content per serving, carrier, capsule count, and trial duration;
- Do not misread “500 mg Meriva® complex” as “500 mg pure curcuminoids”;
- Use extra caution with piperine products when taking anticoagulants, antiplatelets, or other narrow-therapeutic-index medicines;
- For a short symptom trial, record pain and function in advance and stop if no clear benefit appears instead of stacking indefinitely.

## Safety Boundary

Common adverse effects include nausea, reflux, abdominal discomfort, diarrhea, or constipation. Concentrated and high-bioavailability turmeric products have been linked to rare but established acute liver injury. Stop and seek care for fatigue, poor appetite, dark urine, or jaundice.

People using anticoagulant or antiplatelet therapy, preparing for surgery, or living with gallbladder or biliary disease, liver disease, oxalate kidney-stone susceptibility, pregnancy or breastfeeding, or active cancer treatment should check with a physician or pharmacist first. Food-level seasoning cannot be directly mapped to supplement risk, and supplement doses do not imply that more turmeric powder is better.

## Editorial Conclusion

Curcumin should not be presented as a general-purpose anti-inflammatory longevity drug. It has discussable short-term human evidence for knee-osteoarthritis symptoms, while evidence is inadequate for long-term anti-aging, major-disease prevention, or lifespan outcomes in generally healthy people. Product ranking and scientific Tier must remain separate.
