# Intervention Dossier Audit

## Instructions

- `PASS`: the body text and sources can answer the item;
- `PARTIAL`: the item is addressed, but the sources, scope, or detail are insufficient;
- `FAIL`: the item applies but is missing, or there is a clear conflict;
- `N/A`: the item genuinely does not apply, with the reason stated.

Counts are used to identify gaps, not to manufacture a "medical accuracy score."
