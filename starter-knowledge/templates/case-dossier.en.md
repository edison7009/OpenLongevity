## Transferable Practices

Record directions supported by independent guidelines, systematic reviews, or human trials. A source from the person establishes only "they claim to do this"; independent sources are required to judge value.

## Practices That Cannot Be Transferred Directly

- Prescription drugs and medical procedures;
- Supplements with undisclosed doses or products;
- Items dependent on personal conditions, genetics, blood markers, or physician monitoring;
- Conclusions supported only by appearance, photographs, or a single test.

## Safety Boundaries

- Population;
- Contraindications and interactions;
- Conditions requiring testing or professional supervision;
- Uncertainty.

## What This Case Cannot Answer

- Missing health outcomes;
- Possible confounders;
- Undisclosed medical, commercial, or photographic conditions;
- Individual cases do not enter efficacy grading.

## Primary Sources

- Personal/official sources;
- Dated interviews;
- Independent scientific evidence;
- Source versions and local snapshots.
