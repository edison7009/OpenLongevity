# Bryan Johnson’s Day and Week

Bryan Johnson, born in 1977, is an entrepreneur who founded Braintree/Venmo and sold it to PayPal for $800 million in 2013. He launched Project Blueprint in 2021, investing approximately US$2 million annually and using a professional medical team led by Dr. Oliver Zolman to analyze, test, and adjust the protocol. He is currently one of the most extensively measured people in the public record.

## His day

### 05:00 — Wake up

- Wake up naturally without an alarm clock (the body wakes up automatically after the sleep cycle is regular)
- Receive **light** (natural sunlight or light therapy lamp) immediately after waking up to calibrate circadian rhythm

### 05:00–05:30 — Morning Tonic (on an empty stomach)

All supplements should be taken at once in the morning:

| Supplements | Dosage | Instructions |
|---|---|---|
| Blueprint Longevity Mix | 1 scoop | Compound, contains multiple active ingredients |
| Creatine | Extra 5 g (2.5 g included in Mix, total ~7.5 g) | Strength & Muscle |
| Prebiotic fiber | ½ teaspoon GOS + 1 teaspoon inulin + 1 teaspoon arabinogalactan | Gut flora |
| Blueprint Collagen Peptides | 11 g | Skin, Joints |
| Blueprint Essential Capsules | 2 capsules | Contains vitamin D3, magnesium, B complex and more |
| Blueprint Advanced Antioxidants | 1 tablet | Antioxidant Blend |
| Ashwagandha + Rhodiola | 1 capsule | Adaptogens, Stress Management |
| Omega-3 | EPA 400 mg + DHA 400 mg (algae oil) | Cardiovascular, Brain |
| Proferrin (Iron) | 10.5 mg | Iron Supplement |
| NR or NMN | NR 450 mg or NMN 500 mg (choose one) | NAD⁺ Precursor |

### 05:30–07:00 — Training (60–90 minutes)

Bryan's training is not just lifting irons, but **covering all four abilities**:

| Type | Content | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| **Strength** | Mainly compound movements (squats, deadlifts, presses, rows, etc.) | Every day |
| **Aerobics** | Running, cycling, swimming, etc. | Every day |
| **Flexibility** | Stretching, yoga moves | Every day |
| **Balance** | Stand on one leg, balance board, etc. | Every day |

Total training volume per week is approximately **6 hours**.

### 07:00–12:00 — Eating window (all calories are done here)

Bryan's philosophy: "Every calorie must fight for its life."

He eats about **2,250 kcal** and about **130 g** of protein per day, all eaten between morning and noon.

**Typical meals:**

| Meal | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|
| First meal | ~07:00 | **Super Veggie**: A combination of broccoli, cauliflower, mushrooms, garlic, ginger, olive oil and other vegetables |
| Second meal | ~09:00 | **Nutty Pudding**: macadamia nut milk, blueberries, raspberries, chia seeds, cinnamon, etc. |
| Third meal | ~11:00 | Plant-based meals: legumes, whole grains, vegetables, nuts |

Diet characteristics:
- **Almost 100% plant-based** (only exception is collagen peptides)
- No processed foods, refined sugars, or fried foods
- No coffee (no caffeine of any kind after 2pm)
- No drinking
- Each meal is extremely nutritious, "Don't let your brain make your eating decisions"

### 12:00 — The last meal ends

**No eating after noon.** This is one of the core elements of Bryan’s protocol.

His original words:
> "I stop eating around noon, and my resting heart rate drops to about 39 beats/min by bedtime. If I eat 1 hour before bed, my heart rate goes to 56, and my sleep quality is 30–40% worse."

### 12:00–17:00 — Work

- Manage Blueprint Company
- Review biomarker data
- Communicate with the medical team to adjust the plan
- Content creation on YouTube and X/Twitter

### 16:00 — Stop Drinking Water

Stop drinking water after 4pm to avoid waking up at night and interrupting your sleep. "Mineralize" fluids throughout the day - tea, plus electrolytes, or Longevity Mix in the morning.

### 17:00–19:30 — Evening (no food)

- **No fights after 5 pm** ("No fights after 5 pm" - save energy for the morning)
- Use **red and amber lights** at night and avoid blue light
- Turn on night mode on your phone/use blue light filtering software such as f.lux

### 19:30 — Walking outdoors + relaxing

- Walk outside for 10 minutes
- Socialize with family and friends
- Enter the relaxation process: **No messages, social media and screens**, night mode is on

### 20:00 — Bedtime care

- Wash your face and apply skin care essence and hair growth essence

### 20:30 — Fall asleep

- **Bedtime: 20:30** ("Every day starts with the night before")
- Fall asleep within 3 minutes with your head on the pillow
- Target sleep duration: **8 hours** (approximately 20:30–05:00)
- Bryan recorded 100% perfect sleep with wearable device for **8 consecutive months**

> Note: Bryan's current bedtime has been moved up to **20:30** (earlier version was around 22:00). Sleep is his number one priority in life.

## "Late Bryan" Theory

Bryan proposed a practical psychological framework:

> “Evening Bryan, you’re fired. You’ve made my life miserable. I can’t sleep, I’m 60 pounds overweight, and I feel terrible.”

He divided his status at different times into different "personalities":
- **Evening Bryan**: Impulsiveness, junk food cravings, lack of self-control → **Deprived of the right to eat between 5pm and 10pm**
- **Sleep Bryan**: Needs a quiet, cool, dark environment
- **Morning Bryan**: High energy, rational decision-making
- **Work Bryan**: focused and efficient
- **Dad Bryan**: Spend time with your kids

Core idea: **Don’t let your worst self make decisions for your best self.**

## Advanced treatments and instruments

These are high-level projects that Bryan layered on top of the base plan.

### Hyperbaric Oxygen (HBOT)

Bryan calls this one of the most valuable treatments he has ever had.

- Program: 100% oxygen, 2 atmospheres; 60 sessions, 5 times per week, 90 minutes each (20 minutes oxygen + 5 minutes air rest, cycle)
- Equipment: HPO Tech hard-shell cabin, with normal air (21% oxygen) in the cabin and 100% oxygen breathing through a mask
- Note: Avoid cabins with "cabins filled with 100% oxygen" - they can irritate the eyes and easily cause cataracts, have a high risk of ignition and make it inconvenient to take a break midway.
- Results he published after 60 sessions (12 weeks): hsCRP dropped below the test line; telomere length +2.6% (10.3→11.4 kb); SCFA +250%, butyrate +290%; VEGF +300%; vitamin D level +235%; pTAU217 (dementia risk marker) -28.6%

### Intermittent hypoxia-hyperoxia training (IHHT)

- 42 minutes/session as an alternative daily program to HBOT (HBOT 60/90 minutes or IHHT 42 minutes)

### Shockwave therapy (Shockwave)

- 4500 shocks, 3 days a week, intensity level 14
- Parts: Cervical vertebrae, shoulders, ankles, elbows, forearms, lumbar vertebrae, hips and buttocks, knees
- Device: Softwave/Stemwave for active anti-aging

### Mesenchymal Stem Cells (MSCs)

- In March 2024, 300 million bone-marrow-derived MSCs from a young Swedish donor (Cellcolabs) were injected into the knees, hips, and shoulders

### Gene therapy

- In October 2023, receive the first gene therapy: Follistatin (follistatin)

### Cerebrolysin

- 5 mL intramuscularly daily for 3 months for cognition; no identifiable biomarker changes detected

### Sauna (dry steam)

- Dry steaming at 175°F (approximately 80°C) for 20 minutes at 07:30 every day; apply ice packs to lower body to protect fertility
- After 27 ice applications: total motile sperm count +57%, concentration +26%, motility +16%, morphology +15%
- 15 times without ice: motile sperm count -54%, motility -57%, normal morphology -55%
- Bryan believes that high-temperature dry sauna (Finnish sauna, 80–100°C, >19 minutes) has the strongest evidence and unique mechanism, and is superior to wet steam and infrared sauna

### Red Light/Near Infrared Light Therapy (RLT)

- 2 times a day, 6 minutes each time (morning and evening sessions)
