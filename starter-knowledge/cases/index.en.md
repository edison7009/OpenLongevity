# Individual Case Studies

Individual case studies are used to answer two questions:

1. What does this person publicly claim to do over the long term?
2. Which practices are supported by independent evidence, and which are only personal experience, media retellings, or unverified claims?

A case is not a model of guaranteed success, and appearance is not a reliable proxy for lifespan, healthspan, or biological age. A person’s use of a particular diet, supplement, or training method can only identify research candidates; it does not directly prove effectiveness.

## Standard Review Format

Every future case should follow the same structure:

- Identity and information cutoff date;
- 30-second summary;
- Claim-by-claim verification;
- Conflicts between versions from different years;
- Transferable lifestyle practices versus non-transferable personal details;
- Scientific evidence, limitations, and safety boundaries;
- Items that remain unpublished or require confirmation from the person’s own sources.
