# Andrew Huberman’s Protocol

Andrew Huberman, professor of neurobiology and ophthalmology at Stanford School of Medicine and host of the Huberman Lab podcast. His positioning is to use neuroscience to make "executable tools/protocols", and his core belief is: zero-cost tools (light, sleep, exercise, breathing) should be done first, and supplements are only the "last 5%".

::: tip reading method
This page documents Huberman's public tools and protocols for discovering research candidates and does not constitute Open Longevity's recommendations for dosage, formulation, or brand. Content that is not archived from primary sources is marked as `needs-source-check`; supplement recommendations and commercial partnerships need to be read at the same time.
:::

## Core Concept

- **Zero-cost tools first**: Light, sleep, exercise, breathing, social/relationships, and stress management are the foundation; supplements are the icing on the cake.
- **Protocol thinking**: Break behaviors into repeatable "protocols" and execute them according to time periods.
- **Neuroplasticity + Circadian Rhythms**: The essence of many tools is giving the brain the right signals at the right time.

## Illumination Protocol (his most iconic zero-cost tool)

| Period | Method |
|---|---|
| Within 30–60 minutes after waking up | 2–10 minutes of outdoor natural light (more on cloudy days/early mornings) to set circadian rhythm and boost mood |
| Evening | Watch the sunset/low angle light, give "sunset signal" |
| At night | Avoid overhead light/strong light, use warm light/red light to protect melatonin |

## Supplement Protocol, Organized into Time-Based “Stacks”

### Basics / Day Stack

| Supplements | Common published dosages | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Vitamin D3 + K2 | By weight/test, usually 1000–4000 IU | With a fat-containing meal |
| Omega-3 | Heavy EPA, approximately 1000–2000 mg EPA/day | |
| Magnesium | Magnesium threonate or magnesium glycinate | Can also be moved to the sleep stack |
| Creatine | 5 g/day, recommended for almost everyone | |
| Electrolytes (Sodium/Potassium/Magnesium) | Training/Sauna/Morning As Needed | |

### Sleep Stack (30–60 minutes before bed, "Sleep Cocktail")

| Supplements | Common published dosages | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Magnesium (magnesium threonate/magnesium glycinate) | Approximately 145 mg elemental magnesium (threonate) or 100–150 mg (glycinate) | |
| apigenin | 50 mg | |
| L-theanine | 100–400 mg | |

> He emphasized that this set is effective for most people, but it is not needed by everyone, can be rotated, and does not have to be used every day.

### Focus Stack (as needed, not every day)

Caffeine + L-theanine, α-GPC, L-tyrosine, and similar options. He repeatedly emphasizes using them only as needed rather than stacking them every day, and remaining alert to tolerance and dependence.

::: warning Difference from Bryan, Recorded as Stated
Huberman’s public discussion of **NMN/NR** is cautious and does not place it in the foundational stack; Bryan Johnson uses NR/NMN. Both choices are retained as individual case records, while scientific judgment returns to the relevant strategy dossiers.
:::

## Training

- Weekly structure covers **Strength + Cardio + Flexibility**; a common framework is 2–3 strength + Zone 2 + one HIIT/VO2max class per week.
- Pay attention to **exercise timing and circadian rhythm**, cold water/sauna after training.
- Recommend **NSDR/yoga nidra** (non-sleep deep rest) for 10–30 minutes for recovery and stress reduction.

## Sleep

- Set a fixed schedule, cool down, avoid light, and have a pre-sleep routine; like Attia, sleep is the core of repair.
- Use NSDR/Yoga nidra to replenish recovery and reduce stress during the day.

## The general rhythm of the day (summary of public content)

```
Morning Outdoor light 2–10 minutes + water/electrolytes + delayed caffeine 90–120 minutes
Morning basic stack with breakfast (D3+K2, Omega-3, creatine)
Training strength/aerobic/flexibility rotation; cold water or sauna after training
During the day, focus on stacking as needed; NSDR 10–30 minutes to replenish recovery
Watch the sunset in the evening; turn to warm light
Sleep stack (magnesium + apigenin + theanine) before going to bed, cool down and avoid light, fall asleep at a fixed time
```
