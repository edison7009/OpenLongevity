# Peter Attia’s Protocol

Peter Attia is a longevity physician, former surgeon, author of *Outlive: The Science and Art of Longevity*, and host of The Drive podcast. His framework aims to **shift medicine from treating disease to preventing disease and extending healthspan**. Exercise is its primary lever, measurable indicators drive decisions, and his position on supplements is relatively restrained.

::: tip How to Read This Page
This page records Attia’s public framework and practices to identify strategies worth further research. It does not constitute an Open Longevity recommendation of a dose, prescription, or brand. Content not yet archived source by source is marked `needs-source-check`; his relationship with Thorne is disclosed separately.
:::

## Core Ideas

- **Medicine 3.0:** moving from treatment after disease appears (2.0) to personalized prevention centered on function and healthspan.
- **The four horsemen:** atherosclerotic disease, cancer, neurodegenerative disease, and metabolic disease. More than 80% of non-smoking deaths after age 50 are attributed to these four categories, and the protocol aims to delay or reverse them.
- **The centenarian decathlon:** begin with ten things you still want to be able to do at age 100, then work backward to determine what to train now and how much margin of safety to preserve.
- **Five tactics:** exercise, sleep, nutrition, exogenous molecules (medicines/supplements/hormones), and **emotional health and distress tolerance**. He particularly emphasizes that the fifth is often overlooked and that emotional health is an independent pillar, aligned with Okinawan *moai* and *ikigai*.
- **Three-part supplement framework:** ① What is the objective? ② Is there a trackable biomarker? ③ Is the risk worthwhile? Avoid indiscriminate supplementation.

## Supplement Protocol: A Restrained, Test-First Approach

| Supplement | Commonly discussed public practice | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Omega-3 | Emphasizes EPA/DHA and adjusts dose using the **Omega-3 index** | Does not ask only whether it is taken, but whether the blood level is adequate |
| Vitamin D3 | **Tests first** for 25(OH)D; public content often discusses about 40–60 ng/mL and K2 | This records one person’s protocol, not a universal target or dose recommendation |
| Magnesium | Magnesium glycinate/threonate before bed | For sleep and recovery |
| Creatine | Public content often discusses about 5 g/day, including for women and older adults | Interpret alongside the relevant strategy dossier and personal health |
| B12 / methylation-related | As needed, guided by testing | |

Attia has publicly taken a relatively reserved position on “longevity molecules” such as **NMN / NR, resveratrol, and spermidine**, while Bryan Johnson uses NR/NMN. ## Training: What He Calls the Primary Lever, “Bar None”

All four capacities are covered and mapped to the centenarian decathlon:

| Type | Commonly discussed schedule | Intensity/notes |
|---|---|---|
| Zone 2 aerobic work | ~3–4 total hours per week | An intensity at which speaking is possible but singing is not, intended to train mitochondria |
| VO2max | One **4×4 protocol** per week | Four minutes near maximum + four minutes recovery, repeated four times, to train the upper limit of cardiorespiratory fitness |
| Strength | 3–4 times per week | Emphasizes grip strength, eccentric work, full joint range of motion, and leaving margin to prevent injury |
| Stability/balance | Daily | He calls this “the exercise within exercise,” aimed at preventing falls and injury |

## Nutrition

- **Protein first:** approximately 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day, potentially higher when based on lean mass, distributed across meals to reach a leucine threshold at each meal. He has publicly clarified that “high protein causes cancer” is a misunderstanding.
- Avoid dogma: adjust to individual metabolic responses rather than committing to one dietary school.
- **Track ApoB/lipids:** a signature and relatively aggressive view is to use medicines such as statins or PCSK9 inhibitors to push ApoB very low, treating it as a causal factor in atherosclerosis. **This is prescription-level care and must not be copied independently.**
- Limit refined sugar, refined carbohydrates, and alcohol.

## Sleep and Emotional Health

- Maintain a consistent schedule, cool the bedroom, and avoid light. He recorded a seven-episode series with sleep expert Matthew Walker and treats sleep as a core recovery tool.
- **Alcohol:** he publicly argues that there is almost no safe amount and that it severely harms sleep and health; he drinks little or none himself.
- Treat emotional health and distress tolerance as the fifth pillar and actively train them rather than leaving them to chance.

## Approximate Daily Rhythm

This is a synthesis of public material, not an official minute-by-minute schedule.

```
Morning   Light exposure + water + electrolytes
AM        Training (rotate strength / Zone 2 / VO2max / stability) + high-protein breakfast
Daytime   Supplements with meals (D3+K2, Omega-3, creatine, etc.), individualized by testing
Evening   Wind down work and avoid bright light
Bedtime   Magnesium, a cool and dark room, fixed sleep time; no alcohol
```
