# Supplement evidence update — September 2026

This targeted review covers the library's 14 supplements. It asks whether findings change the relevant population, practical benefit, or uncertainty. It is not a systematic review; finding no new outcome study in this selection does not mean none exists. Papers from 2023–2025 are labeled as context or additions to the collection, not new 2026 discoveries.

## Findings relevant to existing strategies

| Strategy | Evidence checked | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| [Creatine](#/supplement/creatine) | A 2026 review of seven randomized trials in postmenopausal women found small lean-mass and strength gains, without an overall bone-density improvement. [Original record](papers/pmid-42141930.md) | Focus on resistance training and muscle function, not proven human lifespan extension. |
| [Soluble fiber](#/supplement/soluble-fiber) | A 2023 lipid meta-analysis of 181 randomized trials is added as context; no new lifespan trial changing the conclusion was included. [Original record](papers/pmid-36796439.md) | Evaluate LDL and other specific outcomes by ingredient and dose. |
| [DHA / EPA](#/supplement/omega3) | A 2025 post hoc DO-HEALTH analysis of 777 participants found changes in several methylation clocks. [Original record](papers/pmid-39900648.md) | A biomarker signal cannot be converted into months of additional life. |
| [Vitamin D3](#/supplement/vitamin-d3) | The 2025 VITAL telomere substudy found approximately 140 bp less attrition over four years; fish oil had no significant effect in that analysis. [Original record](papers/pmid-40409468.md) | Telomere preservation does not establish universal supplementation or higher-dose recommendations. |
| [Magnesium](#/supplement/magnesium) | A 2025 bisglycinate trial found a small insomnia-score benefit; a threonate trial published in 2026 found no between-group objective sleep difference. [Bisglycinate](papers/pmid-40918053.md) · [Threonate](papers/pmid-41601871.md) | Separate formulations, questionnaires, and device measurements; cognitive-age scores are not brain rejuvenation. |
| [Vitamin C](#/supplement/vitamin-c) | New 2026 primate bone-marrow work complements the ACSL4/ferro-aging study. [Bone marrow](papers/pmid-42462722.md) · [ACSL4](papers/pmid-41819088.md) | Mechanistically interesting; the central intervention evidence is nonhuman and does not establish human longevity. |
| [Curcumin](#/supplement/curcumin) | A 2026 umbrella review found osteoarthritis symptom signals with substantial formulation and quality differences. [Original record](papers/pmid-42254374.md) | Symptom management is the relevant indication; superiority to standard treatment or general longevity is unproven. |
| [CoQ10](#/supplement/coq10) | A 2024 heart-failure meta-analysis is added as context; no new healthy-adult lifespan outcome trial was included. [Original record](papers/pmid-39462324.md) | Heart-failure patients and healthy adults are different populations. |
| [NAD+](#/supplement/nmn) | A 2026 comparison in 65 people over 14 days found NR and NMN increased circulating NAD. A contemporary review found inconsistent clinical benefits. [Comparison](papers/pmid-41540253.md) · [Review](papers/pmid-41655607.md) | Raising NAD is distinct from improving function, preventing disease, or extending life. |
| [Spermidine](#/supplement/spermidine) | A 2026 randomized trial reported protection against radiotherapy-associated salivary dysfunction. [Original record](papers/pmid-42460130.md) | A specific treatment setting cannot establish anti-aging benefit in healthy adults. |
| [Ergothioneine](#/supplement/ergothioneine) | A 2026 oyster-mushroom intervention studied memory and mood in 80 people over 12 weeks. [Original record](papers/pmid-42299921.md) | Whole-food effects cannot be attributed to one compound or substitute for isolated-supplement trials. |
| [Glutathione](#/supplement/glutathione) | A small GlyNAC trial is added as context: 24 older adults were randomized for 16 weeks. [Original record](papers/pmid-35975308.md) | Glycine plus NAC is not equivalent evidence for directly supplemented glutathione. |
| [PQQ](#/supplement/pqq) | 2026 papers include mouse survival, a 60-person acute-exercise trial, and a single-arm study with no significant overall AMH change. [Mice](papers/pmid-42132809.md) · [Exercise](papers/pmid-41651893.md) · [AMH](papers/pmid-42500134.md) | More papers do not establish broad efficacy; separate animal, questionnaire, and reproductive outcomes. |
| [Ca-AKG](#/supplement/ca-akg) | The 2026 ITP report found no significant AKG lifespan extension in the tested UM-HET3 mouse conditions. [Original record](papers/pmid-41843349.md) | Include negative evidence; salt forms, doses, and models are not interchangeable. Human lifespan benefit remains unproven. |

## Does this change priorities?

This update does not change T1–T5 priorities on the basis of paper counts, celebrity adoption, or clock scores. Muscle function, lipids, symptoms, disease events, and lifespan are different endpoints. Study doses describe experimental conditions, not prescriptions for every reader.

Papers remain in their original language. Records preserve DOI/PMID, publication date, study type, and a short screening note; full papers are neither copied nor translated.
