## What is more useful than celebrity supplement lists?

Prioritize dated changes, discontinuation reasons, original papers, registered trials, and negative results. Personal statements establish reported practices, not supplement efficacy. Device-derived biological age does not measure years of life gained.

When a complete updated protocol cannot be verified, do not fill in doses or infer current medications from headlines. Fasting, prescription medicines, and gene therapies have distinct eligibility conditions and should not be merged into a universal package.
