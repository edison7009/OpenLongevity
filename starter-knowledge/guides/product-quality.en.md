# How to Choose a Supplement Product

::: tip A brand name cannot replace a product check
One brand may sell products with different doses, raw materials, chemical forms, and certification status. The grading unit should be the brand, full product name, market version, and lot.
:::

## Two Different Axes

| Axis | Question answered |
|---|---|
| Evidence Tier (T1–T5) | How much human evidence exists for this intervention, for whom, and for which outcomes? |
| Product Tier (P1–P3) | Is this specific product accurate, pure, fresh, and traceable? |

A P1 product does not turn a T5 intervention into T1. A T1 intervention also does not mean every product with the same name is worth buying.

## Product Check Order

1. Verify the full name, SKU, country version, and label date;
2. Check active amount per serving, chemical form, and single versus combination formulation;
3. Prefer a specific product verifiable in an independent certification database;
4. Next, look for a COA with lot, laboratory, date, methods, and results;
5. Check category-specific risks such as fish-oil oxidation, heavy metals, and transport temperature;
6. Compare price, taste, and packaging last.

GMP describes a manufacturing system. It does not publicly verify the content, contaminants, or freshness of the bottle in hand.

## P1–P3

| Grade | Standard |
|---|---|
| P1 | Clear product identity and dose, plus independently verifiable product certification or a reasonably complete lot report |
| P2 | Clear product and dose, but gaps remain in current-lot, category-specific, or independent verification |
| P3 | Relies mainly on manufacturer statements, with limited verifiable product-level information |

Leave P1 vacant when no product qualifies. Premium price, brand fame, or public use by a notable person is not a reason to promote a product.
