# AI longevity research — from molecules to human trials

AI helps researchers select targets, design molecules, and engineer proteins. Start by identifying whether a report concerns cells, animals, human safety, or disease efficacy, and whether lifespan was measured. These are representative developments checked on 2026-09-25, not a complete global pipeline.

## Rentosertib: a recent human signal worth tracking

This AI-assisted TNIK inhibitor had a randomized phase 2a trial published in 2025: 71 people with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, treated for 12 weeks. Safety was primary and lung function secondary; liver toxicity or diarrhea caused some discontinuations. [Trial record](papers/pmid-40461817.md)

On September 7, 2026, researchers reported applying six proteomic aging clocks to samples from that same trial, finding directionally consistent reductions in predicted age. This is an exploratory reanalysis, not six independent human studies or proof of longer life in healthy adults. [Clock-analysis record](papers/pmid-42706338.md)

The advance is verifiable human research in AI-assisted drug development. Separating disease improvement from general aging effects, larger samples, long-term safety, and clinical outcomes remain essential. This collection documents research rather than an individual medication regimen.

## Separate the development stages

| Project | Verified stage | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Rentosertib / Insilico Medicine | Published IPF phase 2a trial; 2026 aging-clock analysis | A disease-treatment candidate; this evidence does not establish approval for healthy-adult longevity. |
| AI-screened senolytics | A 2023 machine-learning study validated compounds in cells. [Paper](papers/pmid-37301862.md) | An early discovery example; a 2023 paper is not a newly achieved drug breakthrough. |
| OpenAI / Retro Biosciences | Protein-engineering and cellular-reprogramming experiments announced in 2025. [Announcement record](papers/retro-gpt4b-2025.md) | Reprogramming-marker expression is not human rejuvenation; this source is a developer announcement. |
| ER-100 / Life Biosciences | Registered phase 1 trial in optic nerve disease, primarily assessing safety. [Registration](papers/er100-nct07290244.md) | A reprogramming program; being in longevity research does not establish AI drug-design provenance. |
