## Tier Changes

Broad groupings based on company reputation were replaced with product-level conclusions:

- P1: Sports Research Triple Strength, Puori O3, Carlson Elite Omega-3 Gems, WHC UnoCardio 1000, Life Extension Super Omega-3, and Thorne Super EPA;
- P2: Nordic Naturals Ultimate Omega, Pure O.N.E. Omega, Doctor's Best Purified & Clear, NOW Ultra Omega-3, and California Gold Nutrition Omega 800;
- P3: Jarrow EPA-DHA Balance.

P2/P3 does not mean a product failed testing; it mainly reflects missing public batch, oxidation, or independent-certification information.

Nordic Naturals provides a batch-number COA lookup, but no physical batch number was available in this review. The publicly found IFOS report covered a historical batch, so an old report was not used to promote the current product to P1. Sports Research has similarly named versions with different label values; this page fixes the assessment to `SPRT0048` and requires a field-by-field label match at purchase.
