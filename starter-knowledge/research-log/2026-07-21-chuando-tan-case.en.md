# Chuando Tan Case Creation

Date: `2026-07-21`

## Objectives for This Batch

- Establish a reusable framework for public-figure cases;
- Check a second-hand short-video transcript about Chuando Tan;
- Separate the person's own statements, media retellings, and independent scientific evidence;
- Integrate "cases" into knowledge-base navigation.

## Completed

- Archived three interviews: CNA in 2023, *The Straits Times* in 2026, and Esquire HK in 2026;
- Created the Chuando Tan case page, source-check page, and public-figure case template;
- Labeled claims about eggs, training frequency, sugar, fasting, supplements, mindset, and telomeres individually;
- Returned rumors about NAD⁺, PQQ, and Omega-3 to `unverified`;
- Recorded different breakfast and training versions across years rather than forcing them into a fixed protocol.

## Key Corrections

1. "6 eggs" does not mean 6 whole yolks; the latest interview says only 2 yolks are retained.
2. "Exactly four times per week, 90 minutes each" conflicts with another 2026 interview describing an actual frequency of about three times.
3. "No sugar at all" conflicts with public statements about honey, durian, and occasional ice cream.
4. No reliable primary source confirms NAD⁺, PQQ, Omega-3, vitamins, or a 7-hour fast.
5. The Finnish study is a cross-sectional association; the systematic review finds only a small relationship between stress and telomeres and cannot support "a positive mindset freezes aging."
