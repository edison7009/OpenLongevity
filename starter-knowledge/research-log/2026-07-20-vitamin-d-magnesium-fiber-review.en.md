# Research Log: Vitamin D, Magnesium, and Soluble Fiber

Date: `2026-07-20`

## Completed

- Checked the Endocrine Society's 2024 guideline on vitamin D for disease prevention;
- Checked the NIH ODS professional fact sheets for vitamin D and magnesium;
- Saved 9 PubMed XML records in a batch;
- Checked the FDA regulatory page on coronary-heart-disease health claims for specific soluble-fiber sources;
- Created 3 complete intervention dossiers and 1 paper-batch record;
- Updated catalog status from `candidate/pending` to `reviewed`.

## Key Corrections

- "Vitamin D for people with deficiency" cannot be shortened to "everyone needs vitamin D";
- "Inadequate magnesium intake is common" does not directly imply that everyone benefits clinically from magnesium supplementation;
- "Soluble fiber" must be separated by viscosity, fermentability, and specific ingredient;
- None of the three interventions' clinical uses can be described as "proven to extend lifespan."

## Next Steps

- Create separate sub-dossiers for psyllium, oat/barley β-glucan, and inulin/GOS;
- Add independent evidence for magnesium in migraine and sleep;
- Split future vitamin D work by age, pregnancy, osteoporosis, and confirmed deficiency.
