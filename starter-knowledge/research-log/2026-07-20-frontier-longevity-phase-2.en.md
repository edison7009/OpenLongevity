# Phase Two: Frontier Longevity Candidates

Date: `2026-07-20`  
Scope: NMN/NR → spermidine → PQQ → Ca-AKG  
Status: first targeted review and local archiving completed.

## Why This Batch Was Chosen

These compounds appear frequently in longevity communities, commercial protocols, and high-budget self-experiments. Frequency of use makes them worth studying promptly, but it cannot substitute for human trials. The goal of Phase Two is not to wait until every question is mature before recording it, but to establish dynamic dossiers early enough to preserve potential, negative findings, conflicts of interest, unknown risks, and ongoing trials together.

## Decisions in This Round

| Item | Decision | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| NMN / NR | Combined into one comparative dossier; both recorded as `reviewed / T3` | Both are NAD precursors but are not the same molecule; changes in blood markers are relatively consistent, while functional benefits are inconsistent |
| Spermidine | Major update; remains `T3` | Added a small 2026 immunity/vaccine-response trial, but it cannot outweigh the negative primary cognitive endpoint and long-term unknowns |
| PQQ | Major update; remains `T4` | Added a small single-ingredient RCT; multiple-outcome and product-related issues were identified |
| Ca-AKG | Full dossier created; assigned `T4` | Animal data are attractive; human evidence mainly consists of surrogate endpoints, combination products, uncontrolled studies, or observational data, and the key RCT has not reported results |

## Important Methodological Judgments

1. Use by high-net-worth individuals, founders, or celebrities is an adoption signal, not efficacy evidence;
2. Human research focuses on biomarkers and functional outcomes and is evaluated by evidence hierarchy;
3. Methylation clocks are important surrogate endpoints and must be assessed alongside disease, function, and lifespan outcomes;
4. Personal exploration may be discussed, but objectives, duration, single-variable design, stopping conditions, and medical boundaries must be explicit;
5. All items in this round are adopted in Bryan Johnson's protocol and graded by evidence level.

## Follow-Up Points

- NR: functional and muscle outcomes from `NCT04691986`;
- Spermidine: expansion of the vaccine-response signal and clinical functional outcomes outside cognition;
- PQQ: independent, preregistered, single-ingredient, adequately powered functional RCTs;
- Ca-AKG: formal results, preregistered analyses, and adverse events from `NCT05706389` and `NCT07114536`.
