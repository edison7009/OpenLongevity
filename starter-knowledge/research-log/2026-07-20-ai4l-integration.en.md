## Why Finished AI4L Reviews Were Not Copied Directly

AI4L's examples are complete, long-form English evidence reviews suited to close reading by researchers. Open Longevity's entry point is the general reader. Direct copying would undermine the current "plain language first, evidence second" reading structure and would import expert platforms, brands, and functional-medicine ranges into the product.

The project therefore uses a two-layer document model:

- The main dossier remains concise and easy to scan;
- Audits and research appendices carry completeness checks;
- The original AI4L snapshot is retained in `work/` and is not mixed with our medical conclusions.

## Findings from the First Round

- 23 items passed;
- 15 items partially passed;
- 9 items failed;
- The main gaps were interactions, monitoring, ongoing trials, funding, and conflicts of interest.

These counts are used only to manage gaps; they are not a measure of medical accuracy.
