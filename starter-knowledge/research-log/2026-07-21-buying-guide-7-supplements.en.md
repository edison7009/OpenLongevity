# Research Log: Buying Guide for Seven Supplements

Date: 2026-07-21

## Origin

A practical need was identified for seven supplements—creatine, soluble dietary fiber, vitamin D3, magnesium, NMN/NR, ergothioneine, and Ca-AKG. The existing dossiers described the science but lacked actionable information about brands, use, and the brands Bryan Johnson used earlier.

The scope was then expanded to include public-use records for people other than Bryan Johnson and broader brand research.

## Work in This Round

### First Version (v1)

1. Checked dosage, formulation, and safety-boundary information in all seven dossiers;
2. Checked Bryan's current protocol (snapshot dated 2026-07-20) and confirmed that all current products are Blueprint's own;
3. Attempted to find third-party brands used by Bryan earlier (2021–2022);
4. Could not complete first-party archiving of early protocol pages because network access was limited (Wayback Machine and most external sites timed out);
5. Wrote a practical purchasing and use guide based on existing dossier evidence, the product-quality grading framework, and brand information in public reports;
6. Marked all information about Bryan's early brands as `needs-source-check`.

### Second Version (v2)

1. Checked existing cases (Chuando Tan and Edson Brandão) and confirmed that neither contained verified supplement-brand information;
2. Added public-use records for Peter Attia, Andrew Huberman, David Sinclair, Joe Rogan, and Bruce Ames;
3. Expanded each supplement's brand comparison from 2–3 products to 5–8, covering different price points and certification levels;
4. Added a "Public-Figure Use Overview" table with commercial relationships and source types;
5. Added a "Brand Quick Guide" organized by market position and strengths;
6. Added purchasing-channel notes for the Chinese market;
7. Labeled all public-use records by project rules: first-person protocol/sponsorship/media retelling/`needs-source-check`.

## Decisions

- Brand recommendations are based on product transparency, certification, and formulation, not celebrity use or brand fame;
- Public-use records are labeled separately and do not enter product-quality or efficacy grading;
- Bryan's early brands (Thorne, Nordic Naturals, and others) come from public reporting and still lack archived primary sources;
- Peter Attia and Huberman have brand relationships with Thorne/Momentous and are labeled `sponsored`;
- David Sinclair has commercial relationships with NMN companies, which are disclosed;
- No existing Tier grade was changed.

## Final Decisions

- Creatine P1: Thorne Creatine and Momentous Creatine Monohydrate (verifiable NSF certification);
- Magnesium P1: Thorne Magnesium Bisglycinate/Glycinate and Momentous Magnesium Threonate/Bisglycinate (verifiable NSF certification);
- Vitamin D3 P1: Thorne Vitamin D/K2 Liquid and Momentous Vitamin D3 2000 IU (verifiable NSF certification);
- NR P1: Tru Niagen (standard ingredient in human trials);
- Highest NMN grade is P2: ProHealth NMN Pro and DoNotAge NMN (COAs available but no product-level independent certification);
- Every public-use record identifies its source type and commercial relationships.
