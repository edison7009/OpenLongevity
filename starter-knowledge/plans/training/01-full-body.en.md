# Full Body (1-Day Split)

| Item | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Best suited to | Beginners, returning trainees, or only two to three available days |
| Frequency | Two or three sessions per week, ideally with a rest day between sessions |
| Duration | About 45–75 minutes per session |
| Advantage | Missing one session is less likely to omit a body part, and exercises are practiced frequently |

::: tip Suitable for most beginners
Run this structure for 8–12 weeks. Consider an upper/lower split after technique is stable, recovery is good, and four weekly sessions are realistic.
:::

## Alternating A/B Template

| Session A | Sets × reps | Session B | Sets × reps |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Squat or leg press | 2–3 × 6–10 | Romanian deadlift | 2–3 × 6–10 |
| Bench press or machine chest press | 2–3 × 6–12 | Incline dumbbell press | 2–3 × 8–12 |
| Seated row | 2–3 × 8–12 | Lat pulldown | 2–3 × 8–12 |
| Glute bridge or leg curl | 2 × 10–15 | Split squat | 2 × 8–12/side |
| Farmer carry or plank | 2–3 sets | Anti-rotation press or side plank | 2–3 sets |

## Weekly Schedule

- Two days: Monday A and Thursday B; start with B the following week.
- Three days: A/B/A in week one, then B/A/B in week two.
- Start with only two sets per exercise during the first week and prioritize movement quality.
