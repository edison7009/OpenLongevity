# NMN / NR: Specific Brand and Product Tiers

::: warning Keep Two Things Separate
The **T3** for NMN/NR means the NAD pathway is mechanistically established and under frontier investigation. The **P1–P3** grades cover only labeling, certification, and transparency for specific products. NMN and NR are different molecules and are assessed separately.
:::

## 30-Second Conclusion

- **Tru Niagen** (ChromaDex) is the preferred NR product because nearly all human NR trials used this ingredient, giving it the most complete product-level evidence;
- The **NMN market is inconsistent**, with major quality differences between brands; ProHealth Longevity and DoNotAge are relatively transparent;
- NMN and NR **are not interchangeable**; choose one rather than taking both;
- In 2022, FDA excluded NMN from the dietary-supplement definition because prior drug research existed, leaving its regulatory status uncertain;
- This is a T3 candidate directly adopted in Bryan Johnson's current protocol.

### NR Grading Rationale

**Tru Niagen (P1)**: ChromaDex's Niagen (nicotinamide riboside chloride) is the standard ingredient in human NR trials. Multiple published randomized controlled trials have used Tru Niagen or an equivalent ingredient, aligning the finished product with the studied exposure. It has the most complete product-level evidence in the NR category.

**Elysium Basis (P2)**: independent clinical trials exist for the Basis combination, which contains NR + pterostilbene. Long-term data on pterostilbene continue to accumulate.

### NMN Grading Rationale

The NMN market differs fundamentally from the NR market:

1. **No standard ingredient**: NR has ChromaDex Niagen as a trial standard; NMN has no equivalent unified ingredient standard;
2. **Regulatory uncertainty**: in 2022 FDA concluded that NMN could not be sold as a dietary supplement because drug research came first, although products remain on the market and enforcement status is unclear;
3. **Large quality variation**: products range from specialist brands with COAs to small brands with no testing;
4. **Stability concerns**: NMN may degrade under high temperature and humidity, so shipping and storage can affect actual content.

**ProHealth Longevity (P2)**: uses Uthever ingredient with ingredient-level stability and purity data and provides batch COAs. It is relatively transparent among NMN products, but the COA is manufacturer-provided rather than an independent third-party certification.

**DoNotAge (P2)**: a UK brand focused on longevity supplements that provides third-party test reports. Cross-border shipping, especially in summer, may affect NMN stability.

**P3 brands**: products exist, but verifiable batch reports or independent certifications are missing.

## Category-Specific Risks for NMN/NR

1. **NMN vs NR confusion**: they are different molecules with different studies, doses, products, and safety records and are not interchangeable;
2. **NMN regulatory status**: FDA's 2022 position leaves NMN's legal status uncertain;
3. **Stability**: NMN may degrade into nicotinamide under high temperature and humidity;
4. **Purity**: low-quality NMN may contain synthetic byproducts;
5. **Dose escalation**: studies often use 250–500 mg/day; use trial doses only as reference;
6. **Long-term safety**: short-term trials report good tolerability, while long-term high-exposure data continue to accumulate;
7. **Commercial interests**: several NMN researchers have commercial relationships with NMN companies, including David Sinclair; relevant items are disclosed.

## Purchase Checklist

1. Confirm whether the product is NMN or NR;
2. Read the actual amount per serving in mg, not total capsule weight;
3. Look for a public COA or third-party test report;
4. Observe storage conditions: protected from light and heat, sealed;
5. Buy through an authorized channel;
6. Do not take NMN and NR simultaneously.
