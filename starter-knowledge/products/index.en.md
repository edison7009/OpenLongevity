# Product-Quality Dossiers

This section answers, "For the same ingredient, which specific product is more transparent?" It does not re-evaluate whether the intervention itself is effective.

## Default Purchase Channel

- [iHerb](https://www.iherb.com/) is the default preferred channel;
- Purchase links should point to specific iHerb SKUs rather than Amazon or unknown third-party sellers;
- Brand websites, certification directories, and COA pages remain sources for formulations and quality, but they are not automatically the default purchasing channel;
- A brand website or another authorized channel is listed only when iHerb is out of stock or does not ship to the user's region;
- Price, currency, tax, and availability should be checked on the delivery-region page while logged in on the purchase date.

## Categories Without Product Tiers

- Soluble dietary fiber (to be separated into psyllium, β-glucan, inulin, and GOS first)
- Ergothioneine (T4; too few products to justify a separate tier list for now)
- Ca-AKG (T4; too few products to justify a separate tier list for now)

## Important Boundaries

- Product tiers are P1–P3; evidence tiers are T1–T4;
- Grades apply to a specific SKU, not the entire company;
- Prices, formulas, certificates, and batches change, so every page includes a date;
- Public use by notable individuals is recorded as fact only and does not affect the quality grade;
- Lack of public evidence does not mean a product is necessarily poor; it can only receive a lower grade or remain pending.

See [Supplement Product-Quality Grading](../methods/product-quality-grading.md) for the complete rules.
