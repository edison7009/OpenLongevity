# Seven Supplements: What to Buy, How to Use Them, and Who Uses Them

::: warning Three Things First
1. Brand selections are based on **product transparency, certification, and formulation**, not an efficacy ranking. P1/P2 describe traceability of product quality only; they do not mean "it will work."
2. Public-figure use is **labeled separately by source type** under project rules (first-person protocol, sponsorship, or media retelling) and does not enter product-quality or efficacy grading.
3. All doses are research context or common-use examples, **not personal prescriptions**. Consult a clinician first if you have a condition, take medication, or have special circumstances.
:::

| **Momentous Creatine Monohydrate** (powder) | Creatine monohydrate | NSF Certified for Sport | ~30 USD / 60 servings | Same; brand relationship with Huberman |
| **NOW Foods Creatine Monohydrate** (powder) | Creatine monohydrate | GMP; large manufacturer | ~15 USD / 100 servings | Best value |
| **Life Extension Creatine** (powder) | Creatine monohydrate | High brand transparency | ~20 USD / 100 servings | Middle-ground option |
| **BulkSupplements Creatine Monohydrate** (powder) | Creatine monohydrate | Third-party-testing claim; bulk package | ~12 USD / 100 servings | Lowest cost |
| **Optimum Nutrition Micronized Creatine** (powder) | Micronized monohydrate | Informed Sport | ~20 USD / 60 servings | Athletes; dissolves more readily |

**Key principle**: buy only **Creatine Monohydrate**. HCl, buffered, ethyl ester, and other "new" forms have far less research than monohydrate. Powder offers better value than capsules. "Micronized" only means faster dissolution; efficacy is the same.

### How to Use It

- **3–5 g daily**, dissolved in warm water or juice or added to a shake; with a meal or before/after training;
- A "loading phase" is unnecessary; 3–5 g daily reaches muscle saturation in about 3–4 weeks;
- Body weight may initially increase by 1–2 kg from changes in muscle water, not fat;
- Cycling is unnecessary.

## 2. Soluble Dietary Fiber — T1 (Must Be Split by Type)

### Clarify the Goal First

| Goal | What to Choose | Evidence Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Lower LDL cholesterol** | Psyllium husk | Strongest; ~10 g/day lowers LDL by ~0.33 mmol/L |
| **Lower LDL, food first** | Oat/barley β-glucan | FDA-authorized health claim |
| **Improve constipation** | Psyllium husk | Relatively good evidence |
| **Gut microbiome / prebiotic** | Inulin, GOS, arabinogalactan | Produce short-chain fatty acids but readily cause gas |

| Product | Certification / Quality Signal | Reference Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

| **Prebiotin GOS** | Brand specializing in GOS | ~25 USD | Liquid and convenient |

### How to Use It

- **Psyllium**: start at 5 g/day and increase to 10 g after 1–2 weeks; **take with at least 250 mL water**;
- **Inulin/GOS**: start at 2–3 g/day and increase gradually; initial gas is common;
- Separate from medications by 1–2 hours;
- Bryan's current protocol: ½ teaspoon GOS + 1 teaspoon inulin + 1 teaspoon arabinogalactan.

**Key principles**:
- Prefer D3 (cholecalciferol), not D2;
- D3+K2 is not required; evidence that K2 protects blood vessels remains insufficient;
- If the diet contains enough leafy greens (vitamin K), D3 alone is sufficient;
- **Test 25(OH)D before deciding on a dose**.

### How to Use It

- Generally healthy adults: **1000–2000 IU/day**, with a meal containing fat;
- NIH's tolerable upper intake level of 4000 IU/day is not a target;
- Do not take high doses long term based on an online "optimal level."

## 4. Magnesium — T2

### Brand Comparison

| Product | Form | Elemental Magnesium per Serving | Certification / Quality Signal | Reference Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Thorne Magnesium Bisglycinate** (powder) | Magnesium bisglycinate | 200 mg / scoop | NSF certification | ~30 USD | Lowest gastrointestinal irritation; convenient powder |
| **Momentous Magnesium Threonate** | Magnesium L-threonate (Magtein) | 145 mg / 3 capsules | NSF Certified for Sport | ~40 USD | Marketed for cognition/sleep; recommended by Huberman |
| **NOW Foods Magnesium Glycinate** | Magnesium glycinate | 100 mg / tablet | GMP | ~12 USD | Good value |
| **Doctor's Best High Absorption Magnesium** | Albion TRAACS chelated magnesium | 100 mg / tablet | Uses patented chelate | ~10 USD | Inexpensive |
| **Life Extension Magnesium Caps** | Blend (citrate + oxide + succinate) | 250 mg / 3 capsules | Brand transparency | ~12 USD | Mixed forms |
| **Natural Vitality CALM** | Magnesium citrate drink | 150 mg / serving | Established product | ~15 USD | Palatable; mild laxative effect |
| **Pure Encapsulations Magnesium Glycinate** | Magnesium glycinate | 120 mg / capsule | Hypoallergenic formula | ~20 USD | For sensitive users |

**Key principles**:
- Read the amount of **elemental magnesium**, not total compound weight;
- **Magnesium glycinate/bisglycinate**: least gastrointestinal irritation and a reasonable general first choice;
- **Magnesium L-threonate** (Magtein): marketed for cognition and sleep, expensive, with less mature evidence than glycinate;
- **Magnesium citrate**: acceptable but may be mildly laxative;
- **Magnesium oxide**: poorly absorbed and not recommended as the main supplemental form.

### How to Use It

- **100–200 mg elemental magnesium daily** from supplements, with a meal;
- Evening use may help relaxation, though evidence is not strong;
- The UL for supplemental magnesium is 350 mg/day; magnesium from food does not count toward it;
- People with impaired kidney function should **not supplement magnesium without supervision**;
- Separate from bisphosphonates, tetracyclines, and quinolone antibiotics by 2 hours.

### Brand Comparison

| **Elysium Health Basis** | NR 250 mg + pterostilbene 50 mg | Independent clinical trial of the Basis combination | ~50 USD / 30 servings | Combination product |

**NMN (nicotinamide mononucleotide):**

| Product | Per Serving | Certification / Quality Signal | Reference Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ProHealth Longevity NMN Pro** (powder/capsules) | 500 mg / serving | Uthever ingredient; batch COA provided | ~40 USD / 30 servings | Relatively transparent among NMN products |
| **DoNotAge NMN** (powder) | 500 mg / serving | Third-party test report | ~35 USD / 30 servings | UK longevity-focused brand |
| **Alive by Science NMN** | 500 mg / capsule | Product label available | ~30 USD | Middle-ground option |

**Key principles**:
- NMN and NR **are not interchangeable**: their studies, doses, products, and safety records differ;
- Choose one rather than taking both;
- NR products are more standardized; Tru Niagen is nearly the only NR systematically supported by trials;
- The NMN market is less consistent, so choose a product with a public COA;
- In 2022, FDA excluded NMN from the dietary-supplement definition because prior drug research existed, leaving regulatory status uncertain.

### How to Use Them

- **NR**: 300 mg/day in the morning, fasting or with a light meal;
- **NMN**: studies often use 250–500 mg/day in the morning;
- Bryan's current protocol: NR 450 mg **or** NMN 500 mg, not both;
- Trial doses of 250–500 mg/day are context, not a personal prescription;
- T3 candidate directly adopted in Bryan Johnson's current protocol.

| **Freshly Mushroom supplements** | Mushroom combination containing ergothioneine | Food source | Varies | Not pure ergothioneine |

**Key principles**:
- **T4 early exploration**, adopted in Bryan Johnson's protocol;
- **Prioritize mushrooms** such as shiitake, oyster, king oyster, and porcini, which are natural food sources;
- Supplements are suitable only as an informed exploratory choice;
- Commercial products are generally synthetic or fermentation-derived.

### How to Use It

- Small trials have used 5–25 mg/day; these are research-reference doses;
- If exploring it, start at 5 mg/day;
- Timing is not important.

## General Purchasing Principles

1. **Read the ingredient, not the brand halo**: different products from one company can have very different quality;
2. **Read active ingredient per serving**, not total raw-material weight;
3. **Prefer independent certification**: NSF, IFOS, USP, Informed Sport;
4. A public **COA (batch test report)** is better;
5. **Prefer single ingredients** unless there is a clear reason for a combination; avoid generic "anti-aging blends";
6. **Use authorized channels**: brand website, iHerb, or an official Amazon storefront;
7. **Check expiration date and storage conditions**;
8. **Consult a clinician first when using medication or managing a condition**.

## Notes for the Chinese Market

- **iHerb** is a convenient cross-border channel for US brands and supports Alipay and a Chinese interface;
- **JD Worldwide / Tmall Global** carry some official brand stores, including NOW Foods, Life Extension, and Nordic Naturals;
- Thorne and Momentous have no official Chinese channel and require iHerb or a reliable importer;
- NMN's regulatory status in China differs from the United States. Some products are sold as "food" or cross-border health products, with inconsistent quality;
- Cross-border transport, especially summer heat, may affect temperature-sensitive products such as fish oil and probiotics.
