# Magnesium: Specific Brand and Product Tiers

::: warning Keep Two Things Separate
Magnesium's **T2** indicates conditional use in specific contexts such as deficiency, hypertension, or migraine. The **P1–P3** grades below cover only labeling, certification, and transparency for specific products.
:::

## 30-Second Conclusion

- The main difference between magnesium products is the **chemical form**—glycinate, threonate, citrate, oxide, and others—which affects gastrointestinal tolerability and use;
- First tier: **Thorne Magnesium Bisglycinate** powder and **Momentous Magnesium Threonate / Bisglycinate**, all NSF certified;
- Second tier: **Doctor's Best** (Albion patented chelate), **NOW Foods** (GMP), and **Natural Vitality CALM** (classic magnesium citrate);
- Third tier: brands without independent product-level certification;
- Read the amount of **elemental magnesium**, not the total compound weight.

## Grading Rationale

### P1: Thorne and Momentous

The following specific products appear in NSF's official dietary supplement certification directory (checked 2026-07-21):

**Thorne:**
- Magnesium Bisglycinate, Powder, 1 scoop (3.77 g)
- Magnesium Bisglycinate, Powder, 1 scoop (3.11 g) (another version)
- Magnesium Glycinate, Capsule, 4 capsules
- Magnesium Citrate, Powder, 1 scoop (3.8 g)
- Magnesium Malate, Capsule, 3 capsules
- Calcium-Magnesium Malate, Capsule, 3 capsules
- Magnesium CitraMate, Capsule, 3 capsules

**Momentous:**
- Magnesium Bisglycinate, Capsule, 2 capsules
- Magnesium Threonate, Capsules, 3 capsules
- Magnesium Malate, Capsule, 2 capsules

NSF certification entails content verification, contaminant testing, and label review.

### P2: Doctor's Best, NOW, Natural Vitality, and Pure Encapsulations

- **Doctor's Best**: uses Albion TRAACS patented chelated magnesium, an ingredient with independent absorption research. The finished product itself has no NSF/USP or equivalent product-level certification;
- **NOW Foods**: a large GMP manufacturer with low prices but no product-level independent certification;
- **Natural Vitality CALM**: an established magnesium citrate drink powder with a long market history; citrate may have a mild laxative effect;
- **Pure Encapsulations**: a hypoallergenic formula sold through professional channels, but no product-level independent certification was found in this round.

### P3: Life Extension Magnesium Caps

The blend contains magnesium oxide, which is less well absorbed, and lacks product-level independent certification. Life Extension has reasonable overall transparency, but this specific product's combination of forms and certification status places it in P3.

## Category-Specific Risks for Magnesium Products

1. **Form confusion**: a label saying "magnesium 500 mg" may refer to total compound weight, with only 50–100 mg of actual elemental magnesium;
2. **Magnesium oxide substitution**: some products use inexpensive magnesium oxide (absorption approximately 4%) while claiming "high absorption";
3. **Elemental magnesium vs compound weight**: always read elemental magnesium in Supplement Facts;
4. **Gastrointestinal tolerance**: glycinate/bisglycinate usually causes the least irritation, citrate is intermediate, and oxide is most likely to cause diarrhea;
5. **Kidney function**: impaired renal function reduces magnesium excretion, creating a toxicity risk with unsupervised supplementation;
6. **Drug interactions**: separate magnesium from bisphosphonates, tetracyclines, and quinolone antibiotics by 2 hours.

## Quick Reference by Form

| Form | Gastrointestinal Irritation | Use Case | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Magnesium bisglycinate / glycinate | Lowest | General supplementation, sleep | Preferred |
| Magnesium L-threonate (Magtein) | Low | Cognition, sleep (exploratory) | Expensive; evidence less mature than glycinate |
| Magnesium citrate | Moderate | Constipation support | May have a mild laxative effect |
| Magnesium malate | Low | Muscle fatigue (exploratory) | Limited evidence |
| Magnesium oxide | High | Not recommended as the primary supplement form | Very low absorption (~4%) |
