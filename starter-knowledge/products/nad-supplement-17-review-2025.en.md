> Source: Douyin "Shitouge Nutrition Notes," Episode 1 of a 2025 NAD+ supplement review series covering Australia, Europe, and Hong Kong.

## 1. Why Supplements Use "Precursors" Rather Than NAD+ Itself

Directly ingested NAD+ has very poor absorption for three reasons:

- It is readily broken down by the digestive system;
- Small-intestinal cells lack an effective transporter;
- The molecule is too large.

Most products marketed as NAD+ supplements therefore contain **NAD+ precursors**—ingredients that are more readily absorbed and have a greater chance of being converted into NAD+ after absorption.

### Precursor Conversion Efficiency (Highest to Lowest)

| Ingredient | Conversion Efficiency |
|---|---|
| NR (nicotinamide riboside) | Highest |
| Niacin (NA) | Lower |
| Nicotinamide (NAM) | Lower |

Lower conversion efficiency generally implies a weaker supplementation effect.

### About NADH

NADH is the reduced form of NAD+. In theory it does not require the same synthetic conversion as the precursors above, but:

- Relatively little research exists;
- Oral absorption may be poor.

In simple terms: it may look effective in theory, but the amount actually absorbed and used may be limited, so its final effect could be weaker than that of NR, niacin, or nicotinamide.

## 2. Two Product-Selection Approaches

1. **Prefer NR with a patented ingredient**, followed by niacin and nicotinamide; NADH is optional; **direct NAD+ supplementation is not recommended**.
2. **Examine combination ingredients**, because synergistic ingredients may affect the final result:
   - Support for basic synthesis cycles: vitamins B2 and B6;
   - Support for energy conversion: coenzyme Q10, PQQ, and α-lipoic acid;
   - Support for pathways that consume NAD+: resveratrol, quercetin, trimethylglycine (TMG), folate, and choline.

## 3. Two Bound Forms of NR (Estimated Content)

NR itself is unstable and readily degrades into nicotinamide in water or alkaline conditions, so oral NR must be bound to another compound:

| Form | Binding Compound | Example | Estimated NR Fraction |
|---|---|---|---|
| NRCl (nicotinamide riboside chloride) | Chloride | Patented Niagen® ingredient | 100 mg NRCl ≈ 87 mg NR |
| NRHM (nicotinamide riboside hydrogen malate) | Malic acid | — | 100 mg NRHM ≈ 65 mg NR |

> These fractions are rough estimates by the original creator and are for reference only. Different binding compounds mean equal product weights may contain different amounts of actual NR.

- Among the 17 products, the original creator rated two NR products most highly: **FineNutri Gilded Bottle** (Hong Kong; all-patented ingredients + full-dose combination) and **Swiss Rejuvenation Bottle Ultra** (Switzerland; patented NRCl + patented ergothioneine + PQQ).
