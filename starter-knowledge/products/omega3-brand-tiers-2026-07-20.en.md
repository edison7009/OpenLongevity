# DHA / EPA: Specific Brand and Product Tiers

::: warning Keep Two Things Separate
Omega-3's **T1** means this intervention has substantial human evidence for specific goals and populations. The **P1–P3** grades below describe only whether labeling, certification, freshness, and contaminant information are sufficiently transparent for a specific product. P1 does not mean stronger efficacy or that everyone needs the product.
:::

## Why Were These Brands Added?

The previous version focused on the "general supplement brands" discussed earlier, but fish-oil quality has category-specific concerns: **oxidation, EPA/DHA label conformity, PCBs/dioxins, and heavy metals**. Product-level IFOS listings and batch reports therefore distinguish products better than broad brand reputation.

### Sports Research: One Softgel, High Concentration—Verify the Version First

Nutrasource currently lists several Sports Research entries with identical or similar names. This page assesses version `SPRT0048`, explicitly registered at EPA 690 mg, DHA 310 mg, and total Omega-3 1055 mg per softgel. Another MSC version has different registered values, so the bottle label must match the certification page field by field rather than relying only on the phrase "Triple Strength 1250 mg."

### Puori: Strong Public Batch Transparency

Puori O3 provides EPA 1250 mg and DHA 500 mg per 3 capsules and appears in the specific IFOS product directory. Puori also provides a manufacturer page for looking up third-party test results by batch number. It enters P1 because of transparency, not because a high dose is inherently better.

### Carlson: Established Fish Oil, but This Product Is an Ethyl Ester

The current US label for Carlson Elite Omega-3 Gems provides EPA 800 mg and DHA 600 mg per 2 softgels and explicitly identifies the ethyl ester form. This specific product can be verified in the current IFOS directory. "Ethyl ester" is not synonymous with "low quality," but it is more sensitive to fed versus fasted conditions, so buyers should know which form they are choosing.

### WHC: Very High Concentration, but a Combination Product

The IFOS-registered version of UnoCardio 1000 provides approximately EPA 675 mg and DHA 460 mg per softgel and also contains vitamin D. It cannot be compared as fully equivalent to pure EPA/DHA products. People already taking additional vitamin D should especially combine all sources when calculating total intake.

### Nordic Naturals: Worth Including, but P2 for Now

The current official Ultimate Omega page lists total Omega-3 at 1280 mg per 2 softgels, with a common label of EPA 650 mg and DHA 450 mg, and clearly identifies the form as rTG/TG. The official website also provides a COA lookup using a physical bottle's batch number, which is a good transparency design.

However, no physical batch number was available in this round, so complete results for a current retail batch could not be obtained. Public IFOS PDFs found in search were still accessible but displayed historical validity dates. To avoid treating old reports as current evidence, the product remains P2. It can be reassessed after a bottle batch number and current COA are obtained.

## Why Is Life Extension in the First Tier?

This applies specifically to **Super Omega-3 EPA/DHA Fish Oil, Sesame Lignans & Olive Extract**, not every Life Extension fish oil.

Reasons supporting P1:

1. **Verifiable product identity**: official page provides the full name, Item `01985`, number of softgels per serving, and formula;
2. **Transparent active ingredients**: EPA 700 mg and DHA 500 mg per 2 softgels rather than only "fish oil 2000 mg";
3. **Independent product directory**: Nutrasource's IFOS page identifies the specific product, not merely a badge on the brand website;
4. **Fish-oil-relevant testing scope**: IFOS product information covers label content, oxidation, PCBs/dioxins, and heavy metals;
5. **Clear safety and allergen information**: the official page discloses fish species, sesame, combination ingredients, and anticoagulation warnings.

Limitations remain:

- It combines fish oil, olive extract, and sesame lignans, so any subjective change cannot be attributed solely to EPA/DHA;
- People with sesame allergy must avoid it;
- IFOS verifies quality control, not guaranteed cardiovascular or cognitive benefit for generally healthy people;
- **Mega EPA/DHA, Very Finest Fish Oil, and Super Omega-3 are different products and cannot share one grade**;
- Bryan Johnson's current protocol uses Blueprint Omega-3; this product is not in the protocol record.

## Why Is Thorne Also in the First Tier?

The current official label for Thorne **Super EPA** provides EPA 425 mg and DHA 270 mg per softgel. NSF's official directory still listed Super EPA on the review date:

- NSF/ANSI 173 finished-product certification;
- NSF Certified for Sport;
- Ongoing inspection of labels and manufacturing facilities.

Its advantage is a clear certification identity for the specific product, particularly for athletes concerned about prohibited substances. This does not mean "all professional athletes use it," nor does it mean NSF covers the same areas as IFOS. The public material did not show a full batch TOTOX result, so the P1 conclusion retains that gap.

## How to Choose Within the Second Tier

### High-Concentration Triglyceride Form

**Pure Encapsulations O.N.E. Omega**: EPA 600 mg + DHA 400 mg in one softgel, with good label and process information. The missing element is a current public independent batch report.

### More Transparent Value

**California Gold Nutrition Omega 800**: EPA 480 mg + DHA 320 mg in one softgel. iTested has published batch-number results for content, microbes, and heavy metals. The public report does not fully present oxidation indicators, a central fish-oil quality issue, so "has a COA" is insufficient by itself for P1.

### Common and Easy to Purchase

**NOW Ultra Omega-3**: clear active amounts and reasonably complete process and contaminant-testing claims. Intertek GMP establishes a manufacturing system, not the freshness and content of the current batch.

### Ethyl Ester Is Acceptable

**Doctor's Best Purified & Clear**: EPA 800 mg + DHA 400 mg per 2 softgels. The official page identifies the ethyl ester form and Golden Omega ingredient and claims IFOS. Because a matching record was not captured from the independent certification directory in this round, it remains P2. A current IFOS product page or batch report could support reassessment to P1.

## Why Is Jarrow in the Third Tier for Now?

Jarrow **EPA-DHA Balance** is not low-dose: it provides EPA 800 mg + DHA 400 mg per 2 softgels. The issue is insufficient public quality evidence:

- Current official page relies mainly on manufacturer statements;
- Claims of molecular distillation and compliance with CRN/proposed USP standards lack current batch results;
- No product-level independent certification was captured;
- Current retail label information identifies an ethyl ester, but the official page does not prominently disclose it;
- No public oxidation indicators.

P3 means **insufficient transparency**, not a failed quality test.

## How Should "Who Uses It" Be Recorded?

| Status | Permitted Wording |
|---|---|
| `current-explicit` | A current dated first-party protocol directly lists the full product |
| `current-brand-only` | Only the brand or ingredient is confirmed; SKU needs checking |
| `historical` | Appeared in an old protocol or video; current status unknown |
| `sponsored` | Advertisement, affiliate link, brand relationship, or gifted product |
| `unverified` | Secondary list, forum, retelling, or no original link |

Public-figure use status does not enter P1–P3 grading.

## Checks After Purchase

1. Does the full product name and SKU match this page?
2. Is a serving 1, 2, or 4 softgels?
3. What is the EPA + DHA amount, not total "fish oil"?
4. Can the batch number be found at the certifier or COA page?
5. Was it exposed to prolonged heat during cross-border transport?
6. Does it contain combination ingredients, sesame, fish, or gelatin allergens?
7. Consult a healthcare professional first when using anticoagulant/antiplatelet medication, experiencing bleeding problems, or preparing for surgery.
